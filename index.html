Court-Ready Oregon Pleading Paper & Appellate LaTeX Engine

JustiTeX is an open-source, court-compliant LaTeX & Pandoc compilation package enforcing strict 28-line line-numbered pleading grids, double left-margin rules, and ORAP 5.05 appellate formatting.

28-Line Frozen Pleading Grid

Enforces strict Oregon UTCR 2.010 line geometry, double vertical ruling lines, and exact point-size font spacing.

templates/oregon_pleading_28line_FROZEN.tex
🏛️

Oregon Appellate Compliance

Pre-configured for Oregon Court of Appeals (ORAP 5.05) opening briefs, motions for emergency stays, and supplemental status updates.

justitex compile --appellate brief.tex
🔄

Markdown ➔ Court PDF

Converts standard Markdown legal drafts directly into e-filing ready court PDFs using Pandoc and pdflatex integration.

justitex compile complaint.md -o complaint.pdf
